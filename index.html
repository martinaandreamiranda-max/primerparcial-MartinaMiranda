Sabrina Carpenter: the new pop princess

Born in Pennsylvania, Sabrina began her career in front of the cameras at a very young age. She rose to international fame thanks to her iconic starring role as Maya Hart in the hit Disney Channel series, Girl Meets World. Far from being typecast, she built a solid path in Hollywood, participating in acclaimed dramas like The Hate U Give, teen movies such as Tall Girl, and even starring in and producing successful comedies like Work It. Her charisma and acting versatility laid the foundation for a multifaceted star ready to conquer the world.

As for her music career, her rise has been truly meteoric. In 2023, she cemented her international fame and captured the hearts of massive crowds while performing as part of the acclaimed The Eras Tour. However, her ultimate breakthrough came in 2024, when she released a record-breaking song that completely transformed her career and her life, establishing her as an undisputed global phenomenon. As if that weren't enough, to crown her status as pop royalty, she experienced a historic moment this year by sharing the stage with none other than the legendary Madonna!
